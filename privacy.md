# Musubi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 13 November 2026
**Operator:** Mari Okabe, Victoria, Australia ("we", "us")
**Contact:** musubi.musubi25@gmail.com

Musubi ("the app") is a Japanese-learning app. This policy explains what
information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect your email address. We
don't ask for your name: your account's display name is taken from the part
of your email address before the @, unless your sign-in method gives us a
name. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Clerk (see
section 4), which also processes credentials such as passwords, one-time codes
or social-login identifiers on our behalf, along with the IP address and
device/browser information of each sign-in for session security. When you
create an account we also record, with Clerk, that you confirmed you meet our
minimum age (section 7) and agreed to our Terms of Service, which version, and when. We never see or store your password.

**Learning data.** We store your study activity: which kana, kanji, vocabulary
and grammar items you have studied, your review history and results, and the
spaced-repetition schedule and statistics derived from them.

**Technical data.** Our servers and hosting provider may automatically log
standard request information (such as IP address, device/app version,
timestamps and your account ID) for security, debugging and reliability.

**Crash and error reports.** If the app or our servers crash or hit an error,
a report is sent to our error-monitoring provider, Sentry (see section 4). A
report contains technical details: the error and where in the code it
happened, your device model, operating system and app version, and the recent
in-app steps leading up to it (such as which screen you were on and what you
tapped). We configure these reports to leave out your name, email address,
account ID and IP address.

**Survey answers.** From time to time the app may offer a short, optional
survey. We store your answers with your account and the time you sent them, so
we don't ask you the same survey twice and can understand feedback alongside
how you use the app (for example, which subjects you study). Free-text answers
are optional and limited to 300 characters. Please don't include personal
details in them; we automatically remove email addresses, @handles and phone
numbers we detect.

**Emails to us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and what you
choose to send, such as messages, bug reports and screenshots. We use it only
to reply to you, fix bugs and improve the app.

We do **not** currently collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone
or camera data, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## 2. How we use information

- To create and run your account and sync your progress across sessions.
- To schedule reviews and show your progress and statistics.
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.
- To read and respond to your feedback and bug reports.
- To understand what people think of the app through optional surveys.
- To contact you about your account or this policy (we do not send marketing
  email without your consent).
- To comply with legal obligations.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy
Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

If you are in the EEA/UK, our legal bases are performance of our contract with
you (running your account), our legitimate interests (security and improving
the app), and legal obligation.

## 3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We share
it only with service providers who process it for us (section 4), when required
by law, or in a business transfer (merger or sale), in which case we will tell
you.

## 4. Service providers

- **Clerk** — authentication and user management (email, name, sign-in data).
- **Sentry** — crash and error reporting (error details, device and app
  information).
- **Railway** — hosts our API servers (and their request logs).
- **Neon** — hosts our database (account and learning data).
- **Apple** — app distribution through TestFlight and the App Store.
- **Google** — email (Gmail), for messages you send to our contact address.

These providers may store and process data in countries other than yours,
mainly the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as
standard contractual clauses.

## 5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and learning data while your account exists. You can
delete your account at any time in **Settings → Delete account**. This
permanently deletes your account, learning data and authentication record. It
cannot be undone. Copies in routine server logs, database backups and crash
reports are not removed straight away; they are deleted or overwritten
automatically within 150 days.

Survey answers are deleted when you delete your account. Otherwise we keep
them only while we need them, and no later than 12 months after the survey
closes. You can ask us to see or delete your answers at any time by emailing
musubi.musubi25@gmail.com.

We keep emails and the bug reports in them only as long as we need them to
reply, fix the issue or improve the app. You can email
musubi.musubi25@gmail.com to ask us to delete them sooner.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you
may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict use of your
personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can
delete your account in the app; for any other request, email musubi.musubi25@gmail.com.
We will respond within the time required by law.

**Complaints.** If you think we have mishandled your personal information,
email musubi.musubi25@gmail.com and tell us what happened. We will look into
it and reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response, you may
complain to your local data-protection authority; in Australia, that is the
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined in
California law.

## 7. Children

Musubi is for people aged 13 and over. If you live in the United States, it
is for people aged 18 and over. You must confirm you meet the age for where
you live to create an account. If you are under 18, you need a parent or
guardian's permission to use the app.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or
from people under 18 in the United States. If we learn that an account
belongs to someone below the minimum age, we delete it. If you believe
someone below the minimum age has given us information, contact
musubi.musubi25@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections and
secure on-device credential storage, but no system is perfectly secure.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the
effective date; for material changes we will notify you in the app or by email.
